T-Shirt Math

The 345 shirts at 25 shirts a box take 14 boxes, the last box holding 20 shirts.

Boundary: arithmetic only T-Shirt Math counts an event's t-shirts with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the boxes the shirts take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a shirt, and the minutes the folding takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your shirts, your boxes or your folding table and does not know whether the shirts will really arrive unwrinkled, the sizes will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a box is worth its price or a shirt is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 345 shirts, 25 shirts a box, 45 shirts a week, $118.75 a box and 6 shirts a minute.

The cost truth

The time truth
