T-Shirt Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's t-shirts with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the boxes the shirts take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a shirt, and the minutes the folding takes. It uses no named constants. The shirts, the box size, the weekly use, the box price and the folding speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your shirts, your boxes or your folding table and does not know whether the shirts will really arrive unwrinkled, the sizes will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a box is worth its price or a shirt is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The boxes truth

Boxes: the shirts divided by the shirts a box, rounded up, with what the last box holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the shirts divided by the shirts used a week, with the shirts left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the boxes times the box price, to the cent, and spread over the shirts.

The time truth

Time: the shirts divided by the shirts shirted a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

T-Shirt Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
